# Urban Shadows

Street photography is about capturing authentic moments in the urban environment. This image caught a passerby moving through dramatic evening light, where architectural shadows create natural framing and depth in the composition.

## The Moment

This photograph was taken during the golden hour when the low sun creates long shadows between buildings. The interplay of warm light and cool shadows gives the urban landscape a cinematic quality, while the subject's silhouette adds human scale to the architectural environment.

## Technical Approach

Using a 35mm lens at f/2.8 provided the perfect balance between depth of field and light gathering capability. The relatively fast shutter speed froze the subject's movement while maintaining sharp details throughout the frame.

## Camera Settings

- **Camera**: Canon EOS R6 Mark II
- **Lens**: 35mm f/1.8 at 35mm
- **Aperture**: f/2.8 for selective focus
- **Shutter Speed**: 1/250s to freeze motion
- **ISO**: 400 for optimal noise/quality balance
- **Focus**: Single point autofocus on the subject

Downtown Portland's architecture provides endless opportunities for street photography, with its mix of historic and modern buildings creating interesting light and shadow patterns throughout the day.
