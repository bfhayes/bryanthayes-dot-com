# Forest Path

A serene morning walk through the ancient forests of the Pacific Northwest reveals nature's quiet majesty. This winding trail, carpeted with fallen leaves and bordered by towering evergreens, captures the peaceful solitude found deep in the woods.

## Technical Details

Shot with the Canon EOS R5 and 24-70mm f/2.8L lens, this image showcases the interplay of light and shadow that makes forest photography so compelling. The mid-range aperture of f/11 ensures both foreground and background elements remain sharp, while the 1/60s shutter speed captures the stillness of the moment.

## The Story

Early morning light filters through the dense canopy, creating natural spotlights that illuminate the forest floor. The winding path invites the viewer to imagine walking deeper into this tranquil wilderness, away from the noise of modern life.

## Camera Settings

- **Camera**: Canon EOS R5
- **Lens**: 24-70mm f/2.8L at 35mm
- **Aperture**: f/11 for optimal depth of field
- **Shutter Speed**: 1/60s
- **ISO**: 200 for clean, noise-free detail
- **Focus**: Single-point autofocus on the path's curve

The Pacific Northwest offers endless opportunities for forest photography, with its moss-covered trees, misty mornings, and dramatic interplay of light and shadow creating a photographer's paradise.
