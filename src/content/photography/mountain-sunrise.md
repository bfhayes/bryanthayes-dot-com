# Mountain Sunrise

There's something magical about being on a mountain before dawn, waiting for the sun to paint the peaks in golden light. This image captures that perfect moment when the first rays of sunlight hit the snow-capped summits, creating a warm glow against the cool morning sky.

## Technical Details

Shot with the Canon EOS R5 and 70-200mm f/2.8L lens at 135mm, this image showcases the telephoto compression that brings distant peaks closer together. The f/11 aperture ensures both foreground and background elements remain sharp, while the 1/125s shutter speed was fast enough to handle the slight breeze at elevation.

## The Story

After a pre-dawn hike to reach this vantage point, the wait in the cold morning air was rewarded with one of the most spectacular sunrises I've ever witnessed. The interplay of light and shadow across the mountain faces created a constantly changing canvas of colors and textures.

## Camera Settings

- **Camera**: Canon EOS R5
- **Lens**: 70-200mm f/2.8L at 135mm
- **Aperture**: f/11 for optimal sharpness across the scene
- **Shutter Speed**: 1/125s
- **ISO**: 100 for maximum image quality
- **Focus**: Hyperfocal distance for maximum depth of field
