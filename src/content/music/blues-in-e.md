## The Performance

Captured during an impromptu jam session at The Blue Note, this traditional 12-bar blues showcases the raw power and emotion of electric blues guitar paired with soulful harmonica interludes.

## Musical Elements

The performance features:
- **Classic blues turnarounds** with modern twists
- **Call and response** between guitar and harmonica
- **Dynamic solos** building from subtle bends to explosive runs
- **Traditional shuffle rhythm** with a contemporary edge

## Gear and Tone

- **Guitar**: Fender Stratocaster through a tube amp
- **Harmonica**: Hohner Marine Band in E
- **Effects**: Spring reverb and subtle overdrive
- **Recording**: Direct feed with room ambience

## The Blues Tradition

This piece pays homage to the great blues masters while bringing a personal voice to the tradition. The interplay between guitar and harmonica creates a conversation that's both timeless and fresh.

The energy of the live audience feeds into every note, making this recording a snapshot of blues as a living, breathing art form.
