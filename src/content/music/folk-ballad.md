## The Song

"River's Edge" tells the story of finding solace and clarity beside a flowing river. Written during a camping trip in the Smoky Mountains, the song captures the profound peace that comes from stepping away from modern life's chaos.

## Musical Arrangement

The arrangement features:
- **Fingerpicked acoustic guitar** in DADGAD tuning
- **Gentle mandolin accompaniment** adding Celtic influences
- **Three-part vocal harmonies** in the chorus
- **Natural dynamics** that ebb and flow like the river itself

## Lyrical Themes

The lyrics explore:
- Connection with nature as a source of healing
- The river as a metaphor for life's journey
- Finding clarity through simplicity
- The timeless wisdom of natural places

## Recording Notes

Recorded in a single take to preserve the intimate, live feel. The natural room reverb adds warmth without sacrificing the immediacy of the performance. The mandolin was added as a subtle overdub, weaving in and out like morning mist.

This song represents a return to the roots of folk music - simple, honest storytelling accompanied by acoustic instruments, where the message and emotion take precedence over production.
