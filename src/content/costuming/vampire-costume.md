## Concept Development

Drawing inspiration from classic Gothic literature, this costume aims for authenticity over modern vampire tropes. Think Bram Stoker's Dracula rather than contemporary interpretations.

## Research Phase

Extensive research into Victorian men's fashion provided the foundation:
- **Coat styles**: Long frock coats were standard for gentlemen
- **Color palettes**: Dark colors predominated, with burgundy as an accent
- **Accessories**: Pocket watches, walking canes, and top hats were common

## Construction Process

### Aging Techniques
- **Tea staining**: Multiple rounds of tea and coffee treatments
- **Strategic distressing**: Worn edges and subtle tears
- **Layered aging**: Building up years of "wear" through various techniques

### Custom Details
- Hand-sewn buttonholes for authenticity
- Period-accurate collar construction
- Weighted coat tails for proper drape

## Performance Notes

The costume was designed for both photography and Halloween events, balancing historical accuracy with practical wearability and dramatic effect.

## Final Result

The finished costume successfully evoked the Gothic atmosphere while remaining comfortable for extended wear. The aging effects were particularly effective under both natural and artificial lighting.
