# Live Edge Oak Coffee Table

This project combines the natural beauty of live edge oak with the industrial elegance of welded steel legs. The result is a modern piece that celebrates both the organic form of the wood and the precision of metalwork.

## Project Overview

The inspiration for this piece came from wanting to create a coffee table that would serve as both a functional piece and a conversation starter. The live edge oak slab was sourced from a local sawmill, having been air-dried for over two years.

## Design Process

The design balances the organic curves of the live edge with clean, geometric steel legs. The proportions were carefully considered to ensure the piece feels substantial without overwhelming the space.

### Key Design Elements:
- **Live edge preservation**: Maintaining the natural bark edge while ensuring stability
- **Steel leg geometry**: Angled legs that provide visual interest and structural support
- **Finish selection**: A satin finish that enhances the wood grain without excessive gloss

## Construction Process

### Phase 1: Wood Preparation
The oak slab required extensive preparation to transform it from rough lumber to furniture-ready material:

1. **Flattening**: Used a router sled to flatten both surfaces
2. **Edge work**: Carefully cleaned the live edge, removing loose bark while preserving the natural form
3. **Sanding**: Progressive grits from 80 to 220, ensuring smooth surfaces

### Phase 2: Steel Fabrication
The legs were fabricated from 1.5" square steel tubing:

1. **Design transfer**: Created templates for consistent angles
2. **Cutting**: Precise cuts using a metal chop saw
3. **Welding**: TIG welded joints for clean, strong connections
4. **Finishing**: Ground welds smooth, primed, and painted with matte black finish

### Phase 3: Assembly and Finishing
1. **Mounting system**: Designed removable connection between wood and steel
2. **Wood finishing**: Three coats of satin polyurethane with light sanding between coats
3. **Final assembly**: Attached legs and performed final quality check

## Challenges and Solutions

**Challenge**: Dealing with the irregular shape of the live edge
**Solution**: Created custom jigs and took multiple measurements to ensure the legs would be properly positioned

**Challenge**: Achieving a perfect weld on the steel joints
**Solution**: Practiced the joint geometry on scrap material before welding the final pieces

## Results

The finished table measures 48" x 24" x 16" and weighs approximately 85 pounds. The combination of materials creates a piece that feels both substantial and elegant.

The natural variation in the oak grain, enhanced by the satin finish, provides visual interest that changes throughout the day as light conditions shift. The steel legs add a modern industrial element that grounds the organic wood form.

## Lessons Learned

- Working with live edge slabs requires patience and flexibility in design
- The importance of proper drying time for the wood finish
- Steel fabrication benefits greatly from careful planning and templates

This project reinforced my appreciation for combining traditional woodworking with modern metalwork techniques. The contrast between materials creates a piece that bridges rustic and contemporary aesthetics.

## Future Variations

I'm considering similar designs with different wood species and exploring alternative metal finishes. The modular approach to the leg attachment system could also allow for seasonal leg swaps or customer customization.
