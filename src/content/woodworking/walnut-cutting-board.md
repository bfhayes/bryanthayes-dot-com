## The Project

A classic end grain cutting board showcasing the beautiful grain patterns of black walnut. This project is perfect for beginners looking to practice basic woodworking skills while creating something both functional and beautiful.

## Construction Process

1. **Cut walnut strips** to consistent width and thickness
2. **Initial glue-up** to create a solid panel
3. **Cross-cut and rotate** pieces 90 degrees for end grain orientation
4. **Final glue-up** with careful alignment and clamping
5. **Surface preparation** with progressive sanding to 220 grit
6. **Applied finish** with multiple coats of mineral oil and beeswax

## Why End Grain?

End grain cutting boards are gentler on knife edges and have natural antimicrobial properties. The grain pattern creates a beautiful checkerboard effect that's both functional and decorative.

## Care Instructions

- Hand wash only with mild soap
- Oil monthly with food-safe mineral oil
- Sand lightly and re-oil if surface becomes dry or rough
