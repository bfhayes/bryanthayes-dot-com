# Leuchtturm 1917 A5 Journal Cover

A hand-crafted leather cover for the popular Leuchtturm 1917 A5 journal, built following an excellent pattern from Little King Supply Co. This project combines traditional hand-stitching techniques with modern laser cutting precision.

## Pattern and Inspiration

The design comes from [Little King Supply Co's digital pattern](https://www.littlekingsupplyco.com/), which provides detailed templates and construction guidance. Their YouTube tutorial (linked below) walks through each step of the build process, making it accessible for intermediate leatherworkers.

**Tutorial Reference**: [Little King Supply Co YouTube Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AC4k3oA4bb8)

## Modern Meets Traditional

This project showcases the blend of old and new techniques:

**Laser Cutting**: The pattern pieces were precisely cut using a custom SVG file, ensuring perfectly consistent edges and hole placement for professional results.

**Hand Stitching**: Traditional saddle stitching provides durability and the classic handmade aesthetic that machine stitching can't replicate.

## Construction Highlights

The cover features several thoughtful design elements:

- **Secure journal retention**: Custom-fitted pockets hold the A5 journal firmly without excessive bulk
- **Clean edge work**: Hand-burnished edges provide a smooth, professional finish
- **Functional closure**: Simple but effective closure system keeps the journal protected
- **Interior organization**: Thoughtfully designed interior layout maximizes functionality

## Material Choice

Using 3-4 oz vegetable-tanned leather provides the perfect balance of:
- **Durability**: Thick enough to protect the journal while maintaining flexibility
- **Aging characteristics**: Develops beautiful patina over time with regular use
- **Workability**: Takes stitching and edge work beautifully

## Results

The finished cover transforms the standard Leuchtturm journal into a premium writing companion. The hand-stitched construction and quality leather ensure this will be a lasting piece that only improves with age and use.
