I'm passionate about helping people navigate life's complexities with greater ease and self-compassion. As a Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist, I've had the privilege of supporting individuals, families, and couples through some of their most challenging and transformative moments.

## Who I Work With

I provide therapy services to:
- **Preteens (ages 10-13)** navigating early adolescent challenges
- **Teens (ages 13-18)** working through identity, relationships, and life pressures  
- **Adults (ages 18-65)** seeking individual support for personal growth
- **Families** wanting to improve communication and relationships
- **Couples** working to strengthen their partnership

## My Background

I earned my Master's degree in Marriage and Family Therapy, where I developed a deep understanding of systemic approaches to healing and growth. My training emphasized the importance of cultural humility, trauma-informed care, and the therapeutic relationship as a foundation for change.

Before becoming a therapist, I worked in nonprofit organizations supporting community mental health initiatives, which gave me valuable insight into the diverse challenges people face and the resilience of the human spirit.

## Training & Specializations

My clinical training includes specialized work in:

- **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)** for anxiety and relationship concerns
- **Trauma-Focused Therapy** for processing difficult experiences
- **Person-Centered Therapy** emphasizing your inherent capacity for growth
- **Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT)** for couples and families
- **Family Systems Therapy** understanding relationship dynamics
- **Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)** for psychological flexibility
- **Attachment-based Therapy** for secure relationships
- **Strength-Based Therapy** building on your existing resources
- **Christian Counseling** integrating faith and mental health when desired

I regularly participate in continuing education to stay current with best practices and emerging research in the field.

## My Therapeutic Philosophy

I believe that every person, family, and couple has an innate capacity for healing and growth. My role is to provide a safe, supportive environment where you can explore your experiences, understand patterns, and develop new ways of being in relationship with yourself and others.

Therapy is a collaborative process. While I bring professional expertise and evidence-based techniques, you are the expert on your own life. Together, we'll work to identify what's working, what isn't, and what changes might serve you better.
