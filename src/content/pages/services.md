I offer comprehensive therapy services for preteens (ages 10-13), teens (ages 13-18), adults (ages 18-65), families, and couples who are seeking support for a wide range of mental health concerns. My approach is collaborative, culturally sensitive, and tailored to your unique needs and goals.

## Services Offered

### Individual Therapy
**50-minute sessions** focused on your personal growth and healing journey. Available for preteens, teens, and adults, individual therapy provides a confidential space to explore your thoughts, feelings, and experiences.

### Family Therapy
**50-minute sessions** helping families improve communication, resolve conflicts, and strengthen relationships. I work with families to understand dynamics and develop healthier patterns of interaction.

### Couples Therapy
**50-minute sessions** supporting couples in building stronger, more fulfilling relationships. Using evidence-based approaches like Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT), we'll work on communication, intimacy, and connection.

### Specialization Areas

### **Anxiety & Stress Management** 🌟 *Primary Specialty*
Learn evidence-based techniques to manage anxiety, reduce worry, and develop healthy coping strategies for life's stressors. I work with clients of all ages using approaches tailored to developmental needs.

### **Relationship Stress** 🌟 *Primary Specialty*
Improve communication skills, work through conflicts, and develop healthier patterns in relationships. Whether individual, family, or couples work, relationship health is central to overall well-being.

**Life Transitions**  
Navigate major changes such as career shifts, relationship changes, moving, loss, or other significant life events with support and guidance.

**Family Dynamics**  
Work through family conflicts, improve communication, and strengthen family bonds using systemic approaches that consider the whole family system.

**Trauma & PTSD**  
Process difficult experiences using trauma-focused approaches in a safe, supportive environment that respects your pace and autonomy.

**Teen & Preteen Issues**  
Support young people through developmental challenges including identity formation, peer relationships, academic stress, and emotional regulation.

## Therapy Approaches

My work integrates several evidence-based therapeutic modalities:

- **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)** - Identifying and changing unhelpful thought patterns and behaviors
- **Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT)** - Strengthening emotional bonds in relationships
- **Family Systems Therapy** - Understanding and improving family dynamics
- **Trauma-Focused Therapy** - Specialized approaches for processing traumatic experiences
- **Person-Centered Therapy** - Honoring your innate capacity for growth and healing
- **Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)** - Building psychological flexibility and values-based living
- **Attachment-Based Therapy** - Building secure, healthy relationships
- **Strength-Based Therapy** - Building on existing resources and resilience
- **Christian Counseling** - Integrating faith and spirituality when desired
