# Find Your Path Forward

Life can feel overwhelming, but you don't have to navigate it alone. I'm Hailey Gonnerman, a Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist dedicated to helping individuals, families, and couples find clarity, healing, and growth through life's challenges.

## How I Can Help

Whether you're struggling with anxiety, navigating relationship stress, working through family dynamics, or feeling stuck in your personal growth, I provide a warm, non-judgmental space where you can explore your experiences and develop the tools you need to thrive.

**I work with:**
- Preteens (ages 10-13) and teens (ages 13-18)
- Adults (ages 18-65)
- Families seeking better communication and connection
- Couples wanting to strengthen their relationship

## My Approach

I believe therapy works best when it feels collaborative and authentic. Drawing from evidence-based approaches including Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT), Family Systems Therapy, and Trauma-Focused approaches, I tailor our sessions to your unique needs and goals.

**My specialties include:**
- Anxiety and stress management
- Relationship stress and communication issues
- Family dynamics and conflict resolution
- Teen and preteen developmental challenges

Every person's journey is different, and I'm here to walk alongside you as you discover your own path to wellness and fulfillment.
