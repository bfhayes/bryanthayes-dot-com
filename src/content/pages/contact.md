## Telehealth Services

I offer secure video therapy sessions for clients throughout Oregon. Telehealth offers many benefits:

- Access therapy from the comfort and privacy of your own space
- No travel time or transportation concerns
- Flexible scheduling that works with your life
- Same quality of care as in-person sessions
- HIPAA-compliant video platform ensures your privacy and confidentiality

**Technical Requirements:**
- Reliable internet connection
- Computer, tablet, or smartphone with camera and microphone
- Private space where you can speak confidentially

## What to Expect

When you reach out, here's what typically happens:

1. **Initial Contact** - Call or email to express interest in therapy
2. **Brief Consultation** - We'll have a 15-minute conversation to discuss your needs and answer questions
3. **First Session** - If we decide to work together, we'll schedule your first 50-minute session
4. **Ongoing Support** - We'll work together at a pace that feels right for you

## Important Notes

**Confidentiality:** Please do not include personal health information (PHI) in emails or contact forms, as email is not a secure form of communication. We'll discuss confidential matters during our phone consultation or sessions.

**Crisis Support:** If you're experiencing a mental health crisis or thoughts of suicide, please call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or go to your nearest emergency room immediately. I am not able to provide crisis intervention through email or phone.

**Response Time:** I check email and voicemail regularly during business hours and aim to respond within 24 hours. If you don't hear back from me within 48 hours, please try calling directly as your message may not have reached me.
