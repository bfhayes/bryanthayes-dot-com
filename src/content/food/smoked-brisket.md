## The Art of Brisket

Texas-style brisket is the holy grail of barbecue - a test of patience, technique, and understanding of how heat, smoke, and time transform a tough cut into something transcendent.

## Preparation

The key to great brisket starts with selection. Look for a whole packer brisket with good marbling and a thick flat. Trim excess fat, leaving about 1/4 inch fat cap.

### The Rub

Keep it simple - coarse black pepper and kosher salt in equal parts. This traditional "Dalmatian rub" lets the beef shine while creating a beautiful bark.

## The Cook

1. **Fire management**: Maintain steady 225-250°F using oak for that authentic Texas flavor
2. **The stall**: Around 160°F internal, the brisket will plateau - this is normal, don't panic
3. **Wrapping**: Optional Texas crutch with butcher paper around 170°F
4. **Target temperature**: Pull when the point reaches 203-205°F and probes like butter

## Rest and Serve

Rest for at least 1 hour before slicing. Cut against the grain, separating the point and flat. The perfect slice should hold together but pull apart easily.

## Lessons Learned

Every brisket teaches you something. Fire management is crucial, and patience always wins over rushing the process.
