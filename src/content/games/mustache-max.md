# Mustache Max

*The name's Max, Mustache Max.*

I'm the guy behind the scenes. The guy who gets his hands dirty so that evil masterminds don't have to. With the right connections, you can hire me to setup your dungeon, and heroes will never be tipped off. I ensure MAXimum discrepancy, and speed is my top priority.

## Objective

Work as dungeon maintenance to connect all the lightbulbs. Work fast and get the HIGHSCORE!

## How to Play

- **Move**: Arrow keys
- **Double Jump**: Space bar
- **Controller support is recommended** if you have it!
- **TIP!** Press against the wall when you wall jump to spring away from wall

Master the wall jumping mechanics and speed through each level to achieve the highest score possible.

## Development

This game was created during a game jam with an amazing team. All music and art are original and created during the span of the game jam, showcasing the creative power of collaborative development under time constraints.
